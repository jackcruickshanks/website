This privacy policy sets out how Jack Cruickshanks processes and manages personal data in his role as a local councillor and political candidate. Only Jack and members of his team, acting under his direction, will process personal data.

1. Data controller

The Data Controller is Jack Cruickshanks, Councillor for Arbroath West, Letham and Friockheim, Angus Council. Contact details are available via the contact page on this website.

2. Contact

If you have any questions about this policy, require more information about how we use your data, or wish to exercise any of your rights, please use the contact form on this website or write to Jack Cruickshanks via Angus Council.

3. Lawful basis for processing, source, sharing and security

All processing is carried out under one of the following lawful bases: by consent, under legitimate interest, or in the public interest. These cover processing to conduct casework, campaigning and communication. Where processed under the lawful basis of a task carried out in the public interest, it is to support or promote democratic engagement, and may be used for fundraising activity.

Personal data processed primarily comes from what you have provided directly, together with correspondence with third parties in the course of any assistance given on your behalf. Some personal data may also arise from your participating in a survey or petition. If you do not wish to be contacted by telephone, please do not provide your number.

Personal data obtained in this way may be combined with information from the Register of Electors that councils provide to authorised persons under the Representation of the People Act, and will be used for electoral purposes.

To assist with some matters, or to comply with legal obligations, your personal data may be shared with third parties in the course of dealing with your enquiry — such as local authorities, government agencies, public bodies, health trusts, police and regulators. Any third parties with whom your data is shared are obliged to keep your details securely and to use them only for the purpose for which they were originally provided.

Personal data may also be shared with political party entities associated with Jack — including his party association, branches, groups and affiliates — in order to assist you or maintain contact with you in support of democratic engagement.

Your personal data is used only as outlined here and within your reasonable expectations based on the nature of the communication. Personal data is stored electronically and securely.

4. Special category data

Special category data will be processed with your consent or under the lawful basis permitted in clauses 22, 23 and 24 of Schedule 1 of the Data Protection Act, covering political parties and elected representatives.

5. Transferring data outside the UK and EEA

The EU GDPR adequacy decision means that data can continue to flow between the UK and the European Economic Area (EEA). Some service providers may be located outside the EEA, and it may therefore be necessary to transfer your personal data outside the EEA. Where such transfers occur, they will be subject to contractual terms to ensure your data is protected to the same standard as if it remained within the EEA, and will only occur with your consent.

We will use one of the following safeguards:

  • Where the UK or European Commission has issued an adequacy decision determining that a country or organisation ensures an adequate level of data protection, meeting UK requirements.
  • A contract is put in place with the recipient of the data, obliging them to protect it to the same standards as the EEA.

6. Data retention

Personal data is normally held for a maximum of two election cycles and is reviewed annually to ensure it is not processed for longer than necessary. If it is determined that personal data is no longer required, it will be placed beyond use and deleted.

7. Subject access requests

We will request verification of the identity of any individual making a subject access request, to ensure we protect personal data and release information only to those entitled to it. We may ask for clarification to properly understand a request and will respond within one calendar month of receiving a confirmed, clearly understood request. In accordance with ICO guidelines, we maintain a log of subject access requests.

8. Your rights

Data subjects have the following rights under applicable data protection law:

  • Right to be informed and of access — you have the right to request a copy of the information held about you.
  • Right of rectification — you have the right to correct data held about you that is inaccurate or incomplete.
  • Right to erasure — in certain circumstances you can ask for the data held about you to be erased from our records.
  • Right to object — you have the right to object to certain types of processing, such as direct marketing.
  • Right to restrict processing — you have the right in certain circumstances to request that processing of your data be restricted.
  • Right to object to automated processing, including profiling — you have the right not to be subject to decisions made solely on the basis of automated processing.
  • Right to judicial review — if we refuse your request under rights of access, we will provide you with a reason. You have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office.

9. Cookies

This website uses cookies to improve your experience and to collect anonymous usage data. You can accept or decline non-essential cookies when you first visit this site. Essential cookies required for the site to function are always active. Accepting cookies does not affect how your personal data is used for any other purpose described in this policy.

10. PPERA obligations

Under the Political Parties, Elections and Referendums Act 2000 (PPERA), donations to a registered candidate or councillor above certain thresholds must be recorded and may need to be reported. Donations above £1,500 may be reportable. Donations above £11,180 must be reported to the Electoral Commission and will be published publicly. By making a donation, you consent to your information being used for these statutory purposes. For further information visit electoralcommission.org.uk.

Your donation information will not be used for any purpose other than processing your contribution and meeting any statutory reporting obligations. It will not be shared with any third party except where required by law.

11. Making a complaint

If you are unhappy with the way your personal data has been processed or handled, you have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO):

  • Information Commissioner's Office, Wycliffe House, Water Lane, Wilmslow, SK9 5AF
  • Telephone: 0303 123 1113
  • Website: ico.org.uk/concerns

We retain the right to update this policy at any time. Please review it periodically.
