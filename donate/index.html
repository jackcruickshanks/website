Under the Political Parties, Elections and Referendums Act 2000 (PPERA), you must be on the electoral register in the United Kingdom (excluding the Channel Islands and Isle of Man) to make a donation of more than £500 to a registered candidate or councillor.

If you donate more than £1,500, this may be reportable to the Electoral Commission. Donations above £11,180 must be reported publicly. For more information visit electoralcommission.org.uk

Your information will be used only to process your donation and meet any statutory reporting obligations. It will not be passed to any third party, shared with other organisations, or used for any purpose other than administering your contribution to Jack Cruickshanks's campaign.
