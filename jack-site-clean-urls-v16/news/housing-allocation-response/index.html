Jack responds to Scottish Government housing allocation announcement

Arbroath, 28 March 2026 — Jack Cruickshanks has secured a formal commitment from Angus Council's administration to publish a revised delivery timeline for the £20 million Levelling Up investment programme, following sustained pressure in the Communities Committee over the past three months.

The commitment includes quarterly reporting to the Arbroath Town Board, where Jack serves as a member with direct oversight responsibility. The agreement was reached after Jack tabled a series of written questions and a formal motion requesting that progress reporting be made publicly available on a fixed schedule.

"This money was promised to Arbroath and the people of this town have every right to know whether it is being delivered as committed. Quarterly reporting is the minimum standard for a programme of this size and significance."

Jack Cruickshanks, Councillor for Arbroath West, Letham & Friockheim

Background

The Levelling Up investment was secured for Arbroath in 2023. Jack played a central role in protecting the allocation when it was at risk of being redirected, engaging directly with both the council administration and national Conservative contacts to make the case for Arbroath.

Since his appointment to the Arbroath Town Board, Jack has consistently pushed for the programme's delivery to be subject to proper public scrutiny — arguing that investment of this scale should be transparent in its decision-making, clear in its milestones and measurable in its outcomes.

What changes

Under the new arrangement, the council will publish a revised programme timeline by the end of April 2026, with subsequent quarterly reports covering spend, contract progress and delivery against milestones. Reports will be presented to the Arbroath Town Board and made publicly available on the council's website.

Jack has welcomed the commitment but stated he will monitor delivery closely and return to committee if the reporting standard falls short of what has been agreed. A further update will be published once the revised timeline has been received and reviewed.
